Our Story

The Misal Hub
Recipe

The Misal Hub recipe is a well-kept family secret, passed down with love. Our masala blend is ground fresh every morning. What makes our misal extra special is the three heat levels — each adjusted using our signature tari (spice-laden gravy) on the side.

From a small passion to Mira Road's favourite food stop — every bowl we serve carries years of tradition and a whole lot of love.
