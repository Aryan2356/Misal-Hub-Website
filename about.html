About Misal Hub

Serving Mira Road since 2018

1
2018

It Started on the Street

Misal Hub began as a small roadside stall in Mira Road — a gas stove, a big pot of misal and a simple belief that honest, authentic Maharashtrian flavour would always find its people. Every morning the pot was set up fresh, and every morning it ran out.

2
Soon After

Word Spreads Fast

Word of mouth did the rest. Office-goers, students, families and neighbours kept coming back, and they kept bringing friends. The reviews said the same thing again and again — the misal, the tari and the pav were something special.
